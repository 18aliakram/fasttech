Expert Design & Project Installation

From manufacturing plants and heavy factories to commercial plazas, warehouses, and health complexes, FAST TECH delivers complete power distribution projects. Our engineering team ensures your facility is backed by safe, well-distributed, and reliable electricity grids.

We calculate your exact electrical load requirements, design structural cable tray routes, fabricate standard panelboards, install transformers and switchgear, and verify all systems through insulation and grounding resistance tests before project sign-off.

Our Engineering Services

We provide tailored contracting solutions for all industrial electrical operations.

HT & LT Line Installation

Overhead and underground cable networks, substation transformers installation, towers and insulator sets mounting.

Panelboards & Switchgear

IEC compliance distribution boards (DBs), smart main panels, circuit breakers wiring, and automatic transfer switches (ATS).

Cable Management Systems

Perforated cable trays, structural ladder trays, stainless steel wire mesh trays, and safe trunking installation.

Equipment Commissioning

Setting up, testing, and calibrating industrial online UPS systems, large servo stabilizers, generators, and solar grid interfaces.

Testing & Diagnostic Audits

Insulation resistance checks (megger), earth grounding resistance validation, thermal profiling, and load balancing audits.

Technical Consultancy

Comprehensive system layouts planning, load profiling studies, cable diameter calculations, and grid interconnection approvals.
