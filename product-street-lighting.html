Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our Street & Flood Lighting Solutions.

What type of LED street light is suitable for roads?

The appropriate LED street light depends on the road, mounting arrangement, required illumination, wattage, and outdoor operating conditions. FASTTECHENG supplies LED street lighting solutions for outdoor and infrastructure projects.

How do I choose the right wattage for an LED street light?

Wattage should be selected according to the area being illuminated, mounting height, spacing, required light output, and project requirements rather than wattage alone.

What IP rating should an outdoor LED light have?

The required IP rating depends on the installation environment and exposure to dust and water. The suitable rating should be selected according to the project's outdoor conditions.

What is the difference between an LED street light and a flood light?

LED street lights are designed primarily for roads, pathways, parking areas, and similar outdoor spaces, while flood lights provide broader-area illumination for locations such as warehouses, industrial facilities, and outdoor areas.

Are LED high bay lights suitable for warehouses and factories?

Yes. FASTTECHENG supplies high bay lighting solutions for warehouses, factories, workshops, and other facilities with high ceilings.

What types of LED lighting does FASTTECHENG supply?

FASTTECHENG supplies LED street lights, flood lights, high bay lights, and panel lights for commercial, industrial, infrastructure, and outdoor lighting projects.
