Why Regular UPS Maintenance Matters

A UPS system is your last line of defense during electrical utility dropouts. Without regular preventive maintenance, battery wear, accumulated dust, and loose electrical contacts can cause the UPS to fail when you need it most.

FAST TECH provides structured maintenance routines that ensure maximum runtime, clean power output, and battery life extension. We service corporate offices, manufacturing lines, hospital critical environments, and telecom networks.

What Our Maintenance Includes

We follow comprehensive check lists to ensure every electronic and battery component is checked.

Health & Performance Inspection

Analyzing operating waveforms, voltages, charging rates, log records, and hardware condition indices.

Battery Capacity & Load Testing

Checking battery bank internal resistance, electrolyte integrity, load cell tests, and terminal torque levels.

Cooling System Cleaning

Cleaning filters, blowing dust from circuit cards, oiling cooling fans, and verifying airflow ventilation paths.

Electrical Terminal Check

Verifying contact joints, checking for thermal hotspots, and retightening power cable terminal screws.

Bypass & System Calibration

Testing manual and static electronic bypass operations to ensure load switches safely during emergencies.

Emergency Support

Annual contract clients receive priority 24/7 standby emergency response to resolve system trips.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our UPS Maintenance services.

Why is regular UPS maintenance important?

Regular UPS maintenance is essential to identify weak or failing batteries, dusty fan systems, and worn capacitors before they cause unexpected power failures, system trips, and hardware damage to connected loads.

How often should a UPS system be serviced?

For critical commercial and industrial environments, a basic check every 3 months (quarterly) and a comprehensive diagnostic inspection (with load bank testing and interior dust cleaning) once a year is highly recommended.

What does a UPS maintenance service include?

Our typical preventive maintenance visit includes battery cell health scanning, measuring voltage charge rates, retightening joint contacts, cabinet dust vacuuming, fan lubrication, bypass switch tests, and detailed logging of output waveforms.

Does FASTTECHENG provide Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)?

Yes, we provide standard and custom Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) that cover scheduled periodic inspections, priority emergency breakdown visits, and replacement recommendations at agreed rates.
