Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our Lithium Battery Solutions.

Which lithium battery is suitable for a solar system?

The right battery depends on the solar inverter, system voltage, required energy storage, daily consumption, and backup requirements. FASTTECHENG can help select a compatible battery configuration.

Can lithium batteries be used with UPS systems?

Yes, lithium batteries can be used with compatible UPS systems. Battery voltage, charging profile, BMS, and UPS compatibility should be confirmed before installation.

How do I choose the right lithium battery capacity?

Required capacity depends mainly on the connected load and how many hours of backup are needed. FASTTECHENG can calculate the required battery capacity from the system's actual energy requirements.

What are lithium batteries used for?

FASTTECHENG supplies lithium battery solutions for solar energy storage, backup power, UPS systems, and other applications where efficient energy storage and longer cycle life are required.

What should I check before connecting a lithium battery to a UPS?

Check the battery voltage, charging profile, battery management system (BMS), and compatibility with the UPS. These specifications should match the requirements of the complete backup system.

Can FASTTECHENG help select and install a lithium battery system?

Yes. FASTTECHENG can assist with battery selection, system compatibility, installation requirements, and technical guidance for suitable lithium battery applications.
