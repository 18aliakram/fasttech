Build Standards & Materials

All our overhead distribution materials are forged using premium structural carbon steel and undergo standard hot-dip galvanization to ensure complete resistance against rust, corrosion, and extreme environmental weathering in Pakistan.

Line Infrastructure Materials

LT-HT Over-Head Material

FAST TECH supplies a comprehensive catalog of Low-Tension (LT) and High-Tension (HT) transmission line materials. Engineered to sustain high tensile stress and support heavy overhead cabling networks, our products ensure grid stability and absolute safety.

Our Core Product Range Includes:

  • Cross Angle Iron Arm: Hot-galvanized cross brackets
  • Structure Pole: High-durability structural poles
  • Earth-Stay Rod: Foundation grounding rods
  • Disk Insulators: Porcelain / Composite disk insulators
  • Pin Insulators: Reliable high-tension line support
  • D-Fuse: Dropout cutout fuse links
  • Dead End Clamps: Anchor clamps for cable strain relief
  • ACSR Conductors: Aluminum conductors steel-reinforced
