Online UPS Price in Pakistan

The price of an online UPS in Pakistan varies according to its KVA rating, battery configuration, brand, runtime, efficiency, and installation requirements. Smaller UPS systems designed for offices generally cost less, while high-capacity industrial UPS systems can require a significantly larger investment.

For an accurate quotation, it is important to calculate the required load and backup time instead of selecting a UPS based only on its KVA rating.

What Determines Online UPS Price?

Several factors affect the final online UPS price. The most important factor is the UPS capacity. A 1KVA UPS designed for a small office load will have very different pricing from a 20KVA, 50KVA, 100KVA, or 200KVA industrial UPS.

Battery requirements also affect the overall cost. A system requiring longer backup time may need additional batteries, a larger battery bank, or a different battery technology, such as dry gel batteries.

The UPS brand, efficiency, input and output configuration, installation requirements, warranty, replacement parts, and after-sales support can also influence the total project cost.

What Is an Online UPS?

An online UPS, also called a double-conversion UPS, continuously protects connected equipment by converting incoming AC power to DC and then converting it back to stable AC power for the load.

Unlike an offline UPS, an online UPS does not wait for a power failure before providing protected output. The connected equipment receives conditioned and regulated power continuously. This makes online UPS systems particularly suitable for equipment that cannot tolerate interruptions, voltage fluctuations, electrical noise, or unstable power.

Why Choose an Online UPS?

An online UPS provides continuous power protection for sensitive electrical and electronic equipment. It can help protect equipment against power interruptions, voltage fluctuations, surges, electrical noise, and other power-quality problems. For businesses, this can help reduce unexpected downtime and protect critical operations.

Online UPS systems are commonly used for:

  • Data centers and server rooms
  • Industrial machinery and automation equipment
  • Medical equipment
  • Networking and telecommunications systems
  • Manufacturing facilities and offices

Online UPS Capacity: 1KVA to 200KVA

FAST TECH Engineering Services provides online UPS solutions for different commercial and industrial requirements:

  • 1KVA to 3KVA — suitable for smaller offices, networking equipment, workstations, and other relatively low-power applications.
  • 5KVA to 10KVA — supports larger office environments, servers, networking systems, security equipment, and other business-critical loads.
  • 10KVA to 50KVA — commonly considered for larger commercial and industrial applications where higher capacity and reliable power protection are required.

How to Choose the Right Online UPS in Pakistan

Before purchasing an online UPS, determine the total electrical load that needs protection. The UPS should have sufficient capacity to handle the current load while providing reasonable room for future expansion.

You should also determine the required backup time, since backup requirements affect the size and number of batteries needed. The type of load is another important consideration — servers, industrial equipment, medical systems, automation equipment, and other sensitive loads may have different power-quality requirements.

Input and output voltage, single-phase or three-phase configuration, installation environment, efficiency, battery type, bypass requirements, monitoring features, and maintenance support should also be considered. For industrial applications, professional load assessment and system sizing from an electrical engineering team is recommended before selecting the UPS.

Online UPS vs Offline UPS

The primary difference is how the two systems supply power to connected equipment. An offline UPS normally supplies utility power to the load and switches to battery operation when the input supply fails. An online UPS continuously processes the incoming power through its double-conversion system.

Because of this continuous conversion, an online UPS generally provides a higher level of power conditioning and protection and is more appropriate for critical applications such as servers, data centers, and medical equipment.

Online UPS for Industrial Applications

Industrial environments can experience voltage fluctuations, electrical noise, unstable power, and demanding operating conditions. An appropriately designed online UPS system can provide reliable backup and power conditioning for critical equipment. Online UPS Battery Requirements

The battery bank is an important part of an online UPS system. Battery capacity determines how long connected equipment can continue operating when the primary electrical supply is unavailable. FAST TECH Engineering Services also supplies dry gel batteries for UPS and solar applications, with available capacities ranging from smaller battery configurations to larger industrial requirements.

The correct battery configuration depends on the UPS specifications, DC bus voltage, load, desired backup time, battery capacity, operating conditions, and manufacturer recommendations.

How Long Can an Online UPS Provide Backup?

There is no single backup time for an online UPS. Runtime depends primarily on the connected load and battery capacity. For example, the same UPS can provide different backup times depending on whether it is operating at 30%, 60%, or 100% of its rated load. If you require longer backup, the battery bank may need to be increased accordingly. A professional UPS assessment can determine the appropriate battery configuration for your required runtime.

Online UPS Installation in Pakistan

Correct installation is essential for reliable UPS operation. Installation may involve electrical cabling, battery connections, earthing, bypass arrangements, ventilation, protection devices, load distribution, configuration, testing, and commissioning. For larger industrial UPS systems, installation should be carried out by qualified electrical engineers and technicians familiar with the equipment and its electrical requirements.

Online UPS Maintenance

Regular UPS maintenance helps identify potential problems before they cause unexpected downtime. UPS maintenance may include battery inspection, battery testing, electrical connection checks, cooling and ventilation inspection, alarm verification, system diagnostics, and general equipment inspection.

For businesses that depend on continuous power, an UPS Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) can provide scheduled maintenance and technical support throughout the year.

Common Online UPS Problems

Even high-quality UPS systems require appropriate maintenance and monitoring. Common issues can include battery degradation, overheating, battery charging problems, input power abnormalities, output overload, cooling fan failures, alarms, and electronic component faults.

When a UPS shows persistent alarms, fails to provide backup, does not charge its batteries, or produces abnormal output, professional diagnosis is recommended rather than repeatedly restarting the equipment.
