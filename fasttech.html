About Us

Engineering Excellence Since 2010

FAST TECH Engineering Services is a Lahore-based company providing advanced energy and electrical solutions across Pakistan.

Established in the year 2010 as Electrical Contractor and supplier of Electrical Equipment, FAST TECH has grown to become a trusted name in servicing, Installation and Commissioning of Power Projects, Industrial UPS & Dry Gel Batteries, and HT-LT line contracting.

We are engaged in trading a wide range of quality power products such as Panel DBs, Circuit breakers, Cable Trays, and Cable wires, with an aim to serve the Nation Professionally in the fields of Electrical Engineering.

"Our committed team of professionals work hard to attain complete client satisfaction by offering superior quality range of products and services."

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, reliable, and efficient energy solutions that power businesses across Pakistan. We believe that clients are our strength, and we always ensure we offer the best in every sense.

Our Vision

To become a leading engineering solutions provider recognized for quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction across Pakistan's industrial landscape.

Our Team

Experienced professionals dedicated to powering your success.

Expert Engineering Team

Our team consists of experienced engineers, technicians, and project managers dedicated to delivering excellence. We include specialists in power systems, automation, civil foundation work for transmission towers, and electrical installation. Every member is committed to timely providing quality products and services to our customers, ensuring complete client satisfaction through superior technical expertise and professional project execution.
