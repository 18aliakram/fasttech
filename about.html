About FAST TECH

FAST TECH Engineering Services is a leading electrical engineering company in Pakistan, established in 2010 to provide reliable industrial power solutions for commercial, industrial, and infrastructure projects.

Our Journey & Expertise

As a trusted electrical contractor and supplier of electrical equipment, we specialise in the supply, installation, testing, commissioning, servicing, and maintenance of industrial and online UPS systems, dry gel batteries, voltage stabilizers, HT & LT line projects, transformers, power supplies, cable trays, distribution DB boxes, circuit breakers, cable wires, and other electrical products.

Backed by a team of experienced engineers and skilled technicians, we are committed to delivering high-quality products, innovative engineering solutions, and dependable services that meet international standards while ensuring customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and long-term reliability for businesses across Pakistan.

"Our dedicated team of engineers, technicians, sales professionals, and support staff work together to ensure every client receives reliable products, timely project execution, and complete satisfaction."

Our Mission

At FAST TECH Engineering Services, we believe that our clients are our greatest strength. Our mission is to deliver high-quality electrical products and engineering services that exceed customer expectations while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism, safety, and integrity.

  • Delivering reliable and innovative electrical engineering solutions.
  • Providing premium-quality electrical equipment and power products.
  • Ensuring timely project completion and dependable after-sales support.
  • Building lasting relationships through trust and transparency.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of Pakistan’s most trusted providers of electrical engineering services and industrial power solutions by delivering excellence in every project. We strive to build long-term relationships through innovation, quality workmanship, and exceptional customer service.
