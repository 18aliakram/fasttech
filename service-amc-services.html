Industrial factories, corporate facilities, healthcare providers, and data centers cannot afford sudden power outages. FAST TECH Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) offer a structured framework for periodic system audits, cleaning, load testing, and emergency diagnostics, keeping your power systems ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our Annual Maintenance Contracts.

What is an Annual Maintenance Contract?

An Annual Maintenance Contract is a service agreement that provides scheduled maintenance and technical support for specified equipment over an agreed period. AMC services help businesses maintain equipment performance and identify potential problems before they result in unexpected downtime.

Which industries do you provide AMC Services for?

We provide AMC plans to factories, textile mills, cement facilities, corporate offices, healthcare institutions, data centers, warehouses, and telecom networks.

Why should I choose FAST TECH Engineering Services?

Since 2010, FAST TECH has built a reputation for engineering excellence. We have completed over 500 projects and maintain a dedicated team of certified engineers. Our contracts are customized and offer 24/7 standby support.

Do you offer customized Annual Maintenance Contracts?

Yes, we specialize in tailored contracts. We study your equipment models, redundancy needs, and budget limits to craft an SLA with custom visit intervals and spare-part options.

Why should businesses choose an AMC instead of occasional repairs?

An AMC provides a planned maintenance approach rather than waiting until equipment develops a serious fault. Regular inspections can help identify problems earlier, support equipment reliability and reduce the risk of unexpected operational interruptions.

Do you provide AMC Services across Pakistan?

Yes, our engineering teams are mobile and serve nationwide, managing projects and AMCs for industrial centers in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Peshawar, and Multan.
