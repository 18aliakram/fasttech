Restore Your Backup Power

Unplanned power outages can severely impact commercial operations, data systems, and manufacturing lines. At FAST TECH, we provide complete, end-to-end troubleshooting, component-level card repair, and parts replacement to ensure your UPS units provide smooth power backup when utility grids fail.

Common UPS Problems We Fix

Our engineers have technical expertise in repairing a wide range of common operational faults.

Fault Alarms & Red LED Codes

Constant beeping, display fault codes, red alarm lights, or communication errors indicating underlying board failure.

Backup Power Failures

The UPS fails to provide backup power during an outage, instantly shutting down or dropping the critical load.

UPS Battery & Charging Faults

Reduced runtime, failure to charge battery banks, charging circuit card issues, or battery degradation faults.

Overheating & Fan Failures

System overheating warnings, cooling fan stops running, dust blockages, or high-temperature trips.

Electrical Components

IGBT power module failures, rectifiers, bypass relays, circuit breakers, and control boards repairs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our UPS Repair Services.

What UPS repair services does FASTTECHENG provide?

FASTTECHENG provides comprehensive diagnostic, repair, and component-level servicing for all types of industrial, commercial, and online UPS systems across Pakistan. This includes electrical card troubleshooting, IGBT replacement, capacitor checking, charging circuits repair, and cooling system maintenance.

Where can I find UPS repair services in Pakistan?

FASTTECHENG is headquartered in Lahore and provides professional on-site UPS repair and maintenance services nationwide, serving factories, offices, data centers, and hospitals in Karachi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, and other major cities.

Can FASTTECHENG repair Industrial and Online UPS systems?

Yes. We specialize in online double-conversion UPS systems and large capacity industrial cabinet units (from 1KVA up to 200KVA and above) that protect critical factory equipment and server facilities.

Can you repair UPS battery and charging problems?

Absolutely. We diagnose battery charging voltage levels, replace deteriorated dry gel battery banks with high-performance genuine batteries, clean connection terminals, and repair charging cards.

Which UPS brands does FASTTECHENG repair?

We service all major international and domestic UPS brands, including APC, Eaton Powerware, Emerson, Power Master, Stabimatic, and others, using certified compatible component spares.

How do I know if my UPS needs repair?

Key indicators include red warning indicator lamps, constant alarm beeps, failure to switch to battery backup mode during outages, backup times dropping to zero, or physical signs of battery swelling or heat from the vents.
